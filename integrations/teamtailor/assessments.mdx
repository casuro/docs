## A360 Assessment Workflow
Streamline your multimodal screening process directly within Teamtailor.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create and Launch Assessment">
    Start by creating the assessment in Casuro.
    
    <Frame caption="In Casuro, select 'A360 Assessment', click 'New A360', and choose 'From Job Posting'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/a360-1.png" alt="Create A360" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Select the relevant job posting and click 'Create'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/a360-2.png" alt="Select Job" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Customize your assessment in the editor, then click 'Launch'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/a360-3.png" alt="Launch Assessment" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Automation (Optional)">
    Ensure the integration is active for the assessment.

    <Frame caption="From the success screen, click 'Go to Launchpad'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/a360-4.png" alt="Go to Launchpad" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="In the 'Automation' tab of the Launchpad, verify that the Teamtailor logo is visible.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/a360-5.png" alt="Automation Tab" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Teamtailor Triggers">
    Set up the trigger in Teamtailor to automatically send assessments.

    <Frame caption="In Teamtailor, go to the Jobs page, click 'Triggers' (pencil icon), add a trigger, and select 'Casuro'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/a360-6.png" alt="Add Trigger" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Select the assessment you just launched and click 'Add Trigger'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/a360-7.png" alt="Select Assessment" />
    </Frame>
    
    Candidates will now automatically receive the assessment when moved to this stage.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track Progress & Results">
    Monitor candidate status updates directly in Teamtailor.

    <Frame caption="Casuro syncs real-time status updates to the candidate's profile.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/a360-updates.png" alt="Status Updates" />
    </Frame>

    **Status Updates Include:**
    - Preparing assessment
    - Invitation sent
    - Assessment started
    - Assessment completed
    - Evaluation started
    - Evaluation completed
    
    <Frame caption="Once evaluated, the final score (0-100) and link to the detailed scorecard are synced to Teamtailor.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/a360-result.png" alt="Scorecard" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Case Study Assessment Workflow
Deploy realistic work simulations automated through your ATS.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create and Launch Case Study">
    Generate the case study from your job requirements.
    
    <Frame caption="In Casuro, select 'Case Study', click 'New Case Study', and choose 'From Job Posting'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/case-1.png" alt="Create Case Study" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Select the job posting to generate the case study context and click 'Create'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/case-2.png" alt="Select Job" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Review the generated content in the editor and click 'Launch'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/case-3.png" alt="Launch Case Study" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="'Go to Launchpad' button will appear to confirm the launch.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/case-4.png" alt="Go to Launchpad" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Teamtailor Triggers">
    Automate the invite process within Teamtailor.

    <Frame caption="Navigate to the Job's 'Triggers' section in Teamtailor, add a new trigger, and choose 'casuro.ai'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/case-5.png" alt="Add Trigger" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Select the corresponding Case Study assessment and save the trigger.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/case-6.png" alt="Select Assessment" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track Progress & Results">
    View detailed status updates and scores in the candidate's profile.

    <Frame caption="Real-time status tracking keeps you informed at every step.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/case-updates.png" alt="Status Tracking" />
    </Frame>

    **Status Updates Include:**
    - Preparing assessment
    - Invitation sent
    - Assessment started
    - Assessment completed
    - Evaluation started
    - Evaluation completed

    <Frame caption="Final scores are synced back to Teamtailor, with a direct link to the comprehensive evaluation.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/teamtailor/workflow/case-result.png" alt="Final Score" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
