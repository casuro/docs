## Not getting what you expected?

Since we show full API call logs in the settings page,
we encourage you to check the logs and see if there are any errors 
or any unexpected behavior. We are always there to help you resolve any issues.

## Disabling the casuro.ai integration

### In casuro.ai
- Go to Settings > ATS Integrations > Lever.
- Click on the "Disconnect" button and confirm.

### In Ashby
- Go to Settings > Integrations and API
- Locate the casuro.ai app.
- Click “Disable”
