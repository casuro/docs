## Connection Guide

Follow these steps to seamlessly integrate Greenhouse with Casuro.

### Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have the following:

- An active Greenhouse account
- An active Casuro account
- Admin access to both accounts

<Steps>
  
  <Step title="Initiate Connection">
    
    Start by navigating to your settings in Casuro.

    <Frame caption="Open Casuro and click on 'Settings' in the left menu bar.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/setup/1.png" alt="Settings Menu" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Under the 'ATS Integrations' tab, locate Greenhouse and click 'Connect'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/setup/2.png" alt="Connect to Greenhouse" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Authenticate with Greenhouse">
    
    You will be redirected to the Greenhouse login page. Log in to your Greenhouse account.

    <Frame caption="Greenhouse authentication page will open where you can provide your email (credentials) for greenhouse and click on Login to Greenhouse.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/setup/3.png" alt="Configuration Form" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="You will be asked to authorize casuro.ai to grant required permissions. Check the box and click on Authorize.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/setup/4.png" alt="Greenhouse Marketplace" />
    </Frame>
    

  </Step>

  <Step title="Reachout to account manager">
    
    Once you have authenticated with greenhouse, you will be redirected to the casuro.ai dashboard. Reach out to your account manager to complete the setup.

    <Frame caption="Copy the 'Integration Key' shown next to connected badge and give it to your account manager and ask them to enable .">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/setup/5.png" alt="Copy API key" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

</Steps>

Congratulations! Your Greenhouse integration is now active 🎉
