## A360 Assessment Workflow
Streamline your multimodal screening process directly within Greenhouse.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create and Launch Assessment">
    Start by creating the assessment in casuro.

    <Frame caption="In casuro, select 'A360 Assessment', click 'New A360', and choose 'From Job Posting'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/1.png" alt="Create A360" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Select the relevant job posting and click 'Create'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/2.png" alt="Select Job" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Customize your assessment in the editor, then click 'Launch'. Once launched, this assessment is now available in Greenhouse.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/3.png" alt="Launch Assessment" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add casuro.ai Stage in Greenhouse">
    Set up a dedicated casuro stage in your hiring pipeline.

    <Frame caption="In Greenhouse, click 'Add Stage' to add a casuro.ai stage.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/7.png" alt="Add Stage" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Select the casuro.ai stage and click 'Add'. Note: You need to provide your account manager with the API key to enable the casuro.ai assessment stage. More information is in the setup page.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/8.png" alt="Select casuro Stage" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Give the stage a relevant name and click 'Save'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/9.png" alt="Name Stage" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the Stage">
    Set up the assessment and automation rules for the stage.

    <Frame caption="Edit the stage and select the assessment you want to use. All launched assessments are available in Greenhouse.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/11.png" alt="Select Assessment" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Update other settings as needed and click 'Save'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/12.png" alt="Save Settings" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="[Optional] You can add stage transition rules. Click 'Create a rule'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/13.png" alt="Create Rule" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="[Optional] Once rules are created, hit 'Save'.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/14.png" alt="Save Rule" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track Progress & Results">
    Monitor candidate status updates and scores directly in Greenhouse.

    <Frame caption="Move the candidate to the casuro stage you created in Greenhouse.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/15.png" alt="Move Candidate" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="In the activity feed, you will see notifications from casuro.ai including preparation and invitation notifications.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/16.png" alt="Activity Notifications" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Once assessment is completed, you will see the full log trail of the assessment in the activity feed.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/17.png" alt="Complete Log" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="In the stages section, you will see the final score of the candidate and a link to the scorecard.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/18.png" alt="Final Score" />
    </Frame>
    <br/>
    <Frame caption="Clicking the scorecard link directs you to the detailed casuro.ai scorecard.">
      <img src="/images/integrations/greenhouse/workflow/19.png" alt="Scorecard" />
    </Frame>

    **Status Updates Include:**
    - Preparing assessment
    - Invitation sent
    - Assessment started
    - Assessment completed
    - Evaluation started
    - Evaluation completed
  </Step>
</Steps>
