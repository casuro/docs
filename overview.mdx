We keep things simple and super easy to use. So lets begin with some basic concepts of casuro.ai.

## Assessments

The assessments in casuro.ai follow the following lifecycle

<Steps>
<Step title="Create or Edit" icon="laptop-minimal">

This is where it all begins. In casuro.ai application, on the left sidebar you will find `Assessments` section.
This is where we create or edit all the assessments while collaborating with your hiring team as well as your AI coworkers.

</Step>

<Step title="Launchpad" icon="rocket">

Once the hiring team is satisfied with the assessment, they can launch it.
This will create a launchpad for the assessment that is ready to share with candidates.
All the launched assessment are available in the `Launchpad` section of the left sidebar.

</Step>

<Step title="Challenge" icon="biceps-flexed">

A challenge is a live instance of an assessment that is ready to be taken by candidates.
So a challenge is tied to a candidate. A launched assessment can have multiple challenges.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Interview platform

We provide an interview platform to facilitate the interview process with <Badge color="blue">state-of-the-art</Badge> AI features. 

Unlike Zoom, Google Meet, MS Teams etc which are general purpose video conferencing tools, 
casuro.ai provides an interview platform that is specifically designed for the interview process keeping bar raiser and AI agents in mind.
