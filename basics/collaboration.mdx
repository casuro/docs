## Inviting your team

<Info>
**Pre-requisites**

- Active casuro.ai account 
- Admin or owner privileges
</Info>

To invite your team to your casuro.ai account, follow these steps:

<Steps>
<Step title="Go to manage organization">

<Frame
caption="Click on the organization icon located at bottom left sidebar. Then click on manage to open the manage form."
>
  <img src="/images/basics/collaboration/step1.png" alt="Descriptive alt text" />
</Frame>

</Step>
<Step title="Click on invite button">
<Frame
caption="Click on members on the sidebar. Then, on the invite button to open invitation form."
>
  <img 
  src="/images/basics/collaboration/step2.png" 
  alt="Descriptive alt text" 
  />
</Frame>
</Step>
<Step title="Send the invite">
<Frame
caption="Enter the email(s) in the form. Then select the role for the user(s). Then click on send invitation."
>
  <img 
  src="/images/basics/collaboration/step3.png" 
  alt="Descriptive alt text" 
  />
</Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>

Once the invite is sent, the user will receive an email with a link to accept the invite.

<Note>
Sometimes the email may not reach the user's inbox. In that case, let the invited user know to check the spam folder
</Note>

## Permissions

Admins and oweners can change the role attached to a user at any point in time. 
So if you've sent an invite to a user and want to change their role, you can do that easily.
