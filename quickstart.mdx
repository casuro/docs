## Get started

<Callout icon="star">
Signing up to casuro.ai takes seconds. Sign in via email or SSO, create your organization, and you’re ready to go.
</Callout>

## Create an account
The first step to use casuro.ai is creating an account. Click the “Go to app” button at the top right hand corner of the opening page to get started.

<Tip>
Since casuro.ai is a business to business product, you will need an organization email to create an account.
</Tip>

You have three sign-in options: 

- Continue with Google
- Continue with Email
- Continue with SSO / SAML <Badge color="yellow">Enterprise Plan</Badge>

<Frame>
  <img 
    src="/images/quickstart/signin.png" 
    alt="casuro.ai sign in" 
  />
</Frame>

<Info>
SSO / SAML is available on the enterprise plan. Please reach out to support@casuro.ai to get it set up.
</Info>


## Create your organization

After you sign in, you’ll be asked to create an organization name if you are the first user in your organization to sign up.
If not, you will be provided with the detailed instructions to contact your organization admin to grant you necessary permissions.
This name is for internal use only. Most customers use their company or team name (for example, Pied Piper or Pied Piper Recruiting).

<Frame>
  <img 
    src="/images/quickstart/create-organization.png" 
    alt="casuro.ai organization" 
  />
</Frame>

You can update the organization name at any time, so it’s okay if you’re unsure right now.

## That's it! You're ready to hire

After creating or joining your organization, you will taken to casuro.ai app where you will be greeted by an hands on tour to get you started.
